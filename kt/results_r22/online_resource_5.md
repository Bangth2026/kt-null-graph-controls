The worlds differ in kind, so the main text pools no interval over them; the pooled values are given at the end of this section for comparison with the first revision.

# S19 Estimates, intervals and power of every comparison

First attempts at a KC, 10% of the training learners, update budget (`kt/src/r22_stats.py`). Corpus mean: mean over corpora of the subsample means. Mixed: random intercept per corpus. *t*: interval over corpus means with df = corpora − 1. Bootstrap: 2,000 resamples of corpora, then of units within corpora. MDE = (*t*~0.975,df~ + *t*~0.80,df~) SE with the standard error of the mixed model and df = corpora − 1; with ten corpora the factor is 3.15 instead of 2.80 for normal quantiles. Terms prefixed other/ are the DKT conditional-influence graph (DKT) and the co-performance graph (corr) of release 4.1.0; DP(DKT) is the degree-preserving rewiring of DKT.
