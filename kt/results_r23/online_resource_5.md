# S17 Simulated worlds and the positive control

Learners are generated by `kt/src/r20_sim.py` (sim-DBE, sim-A09) and `kt/src/r22_sim.py` (sim-DBE-mix; world parameters and true graph copied from sim-DBE, each learner's curriculum position perturbed by Gaussian noise with standard deviation K/3, drawn from a separate random stream). The tracers are trained by `kt/src/r21_controls.py` under the update budget, graph in the embedding refinement. The worlds differ in kind, so no interval is pooled over worlds; Table S17.6 pools over training sizes within each world.

# S19 Estimates, intervals and power of every comparison

First attempts at a KC, 10% of the training learners, update budget (`kt/src/r22_stats.py`). Corpus mean: mean over corpora of the subsample means. Mixed: random intercept per corpus. *t*: interval over corpus means with df = corpora − 1. Bootstrap: 2,000 resamples of corpora, then of units within corpora. MDE = (*t*~0.975,df~ + *t*~0.80,df~) SE with the standard error of the mixed model and df = corpora − 1; with ten corpora the factor is 3.15 instead of 2.80 for normal quantiles. Transfer terms (one subsample per corpus) are given once, on the 32-unit set; the 20-unit set of version 8.0.0 contained the same runs. Terms prefixed other/ are the DKT conditional-influence graph (DKT) and the co-performance graph (corr) of release 4.1.0; DP(DKT) is the degree-preserving rewiring of DKT.
